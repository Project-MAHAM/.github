# MAHAM

### Modules for Astroparticle, High-energy, Astronomy and Multimessenger

**MAHAM** is an open, community-developed scientific toolkit for reusable and validated tools, models, methods, and analysis utilities across astroparticle physics, high-energy astrophysics, astronomy, and multimessenger research.

Our goal is to turn scientific calculations that are repeatedly reimplemented across experiments and research groups into transparent, tested, documented, validated, and citable community resources.

## What MAHAM aims to provide

MAHAM will include reusable components for areas such as:

- particle and astroparticle physics
- neutrino, cosmic-ray, and gamma-ray physics
- physical and astrophysical models
- fluxes, spectra, and source populations
- particle interactions and propagation
- statistical methods and limits
- detector acceptance, exposure, and sensitivity
- astronomical calculations and source visibility
- multimessenger analyses
- radio-frequency and antenna-related calculations
- scientific visualization and data utilities

## Principles

- **Modular:** individual tools and models should be useful independently.
- **Scientifically validated:** implementations should be checked against analytical results, published calculations, established software, or suitable reference data.
- **Transparent:** equations, assumptions, conventions, units, validity ranges, and references should be documented.
- **Interoperable:** MAHAM should complement existing scientific software rather than replace it.
- **Citable:** scientific models and methods should retain their original references, while MAHAM releases provide stable software citations.
- **Community-developed:** contributions, validation studies, documentation, and expert scientific review are encouraged.

## What MAHAM is not

MAHAM is not intended to be an experiment-specific detector simulation or event-reconstruction framework.

Instead, it focuses on portable scientific building blocks that can be used independently or alongside existing software.

## Status

**MAHAM is currently under active development.**

The initial software architecture, scientific scope, validation standards, and contributor model are being established.

Researchers interested in contributing scientific modules, models, validation cases, documentation, or expert review are welcome to participate.
